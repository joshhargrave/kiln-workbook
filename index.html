Save to your phone

one-tap access

Add the workbook to your home screen and it opens full screen like an app. Pick your device:

iPhone / iPadSafari
  1. Tap the ••• (More) button in the bottom-right of Safari.
  2. Tap Share — the square with an arrow pointing up out of it.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen — the square with a + inside.
  4. Tap Add. The Kiln Workbook icon lands on your home screen.

Use Safari for this. On older iPhones the Share icon sits right in the toolbar, so you can skip the ••• step.

AndroidChrome
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top-right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add / Install.

Some phones show an install banner you can tap instead.

Computerbookmark
  1. Press Ctrl + D (Windows) or ⌘ + D (Mac) to bookmark it.
  2. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar to open it in its own window.

The bookmark keeps its Kiln Workbook icon in your bar.
